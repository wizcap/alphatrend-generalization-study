# Research preprint v1.0.0

**Generalization Limits of Adaptive AlphaTrend Exit Models in Cryptocurrency Futures**

This initial public release contains the 12-page paper, Traditional Chinese conclusions, frozen source and tests, 768 research model versions, complete study015 results and derived account curves, source archive hashes, and public reproduction tools.

The comparison found period-dependent tradeoffs but no adjusted development interval entirely above zero. No replacement model was selected for deployment. All evaluation history had already been inspected; this is a discovery-only study, not a fresh holdout or evidence of live profitability.

The publication records 303 passing tests, 704 independent model refits, 40 regenerated intervals, and raw-market replay of 720 asset accounts. Detailed check scope and limitations are in `PUBLIC_VALIDATION.json`.

The PDF is attached below. Citation metadata is in `CITATION.cff`; source is MPL-2.0, and the original paper and figures are CC BY 4.0. **This is a preprint, not peer reviewed. No journal acceptance or DOI is claimed.**
